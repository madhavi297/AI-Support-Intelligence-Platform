# Billing and Subscription Policies

## Section 1: Double Charges and Duplicate Transactions
If a customer notices duplicate or identical charges on their credit card statement, this is typically due to an authorization hold from the issuing bank or multiple click attempts during checkout. 
Our billing department will immediately verify transaction IDs in Stripe and initiate a full refund for the duplicate transaction. Refunds take 3 to 5 business days to appear on the customer's card statement. We do not charge transaction dispute fees when resolved directly with support.

## Section 2: Refund Policy and SLA Downtime Credits
Refunds for monthly subscriptions are provided on a prorated basis within 14 days of renewal if no significant API usage occurred during the period. 
In the event of an unplanned service outage exceeding our 99.9% monthly uptime guarantee, customers on Enterprise or Business plans are eligible for a 10% SLA credit upon request within 30 days of the incident. SLA credits are applied to the next billing invoice.

## Section 3: Payment Failures and Card Declines
When an automatic credit card or ACH bank payment fails, our billing system attempts up to 3 automatic retries over a 7-day grace period. 
To resolve credit card declines (such as error code ERR_CARD_DECLINED or ERR_ACH_501), the account administrator must visit Billing Settings > Payment Methods and enter a valid card with 3D Secure enabled. Service access remains active during the grace period.

## Section 4: Subscription Cancellation and Downgrades
Customers can cancel their subscription or downgrade to the Free tier at any time through the Billing Portal. 
Upon cancellation, access to paid features continues until the end of the current paid billing period. We do not automatically terminate access mid-cycle, and no partial refunds are issued for early cancellation unless required by local law.

## Section 5: Invoices, Receipts, and Tax Exemption
All automated invoices and VAT receipts are available for PDF download in the Billing History section. 
Educational institutions, government agencies, and registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organizations eligible for sales tax exemption can submit their Tax Exemption Certificate to billing-exemptions@platform.com to remove taxes from future recurring invoices.
